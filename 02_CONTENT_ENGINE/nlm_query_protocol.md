# NLM Query Protocol — Hard-Baked Rule

## Absolute Rule

Every NLM query MUST be:
1. **Bible-first** — Start with "From Scripture alone:" or "What does the Bible teach about..."
2. **Open-ended** — One open question. No lists of passages. No pre-structured sections.
3. **No application pre-framing** — Do not say "and how does this apply to X, Y, Z"
4. **No tradition-first framing** — Do not start with "What does the Reformed tradition say..."

## Why

Pre-structuring queries loads the answer with human framework instead of letting NLM develop God's framework. This produces weaker theology and has been corrected multiple times.

## Violation Patterns (Never)

- Listing specific passages: "Matthew 7:3-5, Luke 18:9-14"
- Pre-structuring sections: "cover: 1. biblical texts 2. Reformed view 3. modern application"
- Tradition-first framing: "What does the Reformed tradition say about..."
- Directive application: "How does this apply to social media, politics..."
- Queries longer than 2 sentences

## Correct Pattern (Copy This)

- "From Scripture alone: what does God reveal about [topic]?"
- "What does the Bible teach about [topic]?"
- "How should believers understand [topic] in light of God's Word?"

One sentence. Open question. Scripture first. No lists. No pre-structure.
