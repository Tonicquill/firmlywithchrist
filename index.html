Mozambique: Beheaded for Refusing to Convert. To Die Is Gain.

Islamic State Mozambique militants beheaded two Christians and killed five more in Cabo Delgado for refusing to renounce their faith. IS-M now calls Christians who refuse conversion 'combatants.' The church has a different name for them.

Persecution Matthew 10:28 Revelation 2:10 Revelation 6:10
Read below ↓

All Scripture is breathed out by God and profitable for teaching, for reproof, for correction, and for training in righteousness.

2 Timothy 3:16

On May 9, Islamic State Mozambique militants stormed the village of Namecala in Ancuabe District, Cabo Delgado. They killed five Christians, burned a church building, and torched more than one hundred sixty homes. The day before, two Christians were captured and beheaded near the same village. A day earlier, another believer was killed near Nanoni. The group — known locally as al-Shabaab, unrelated to the Somali organization — has pledged allegiance to Islamic State and now calls Christians who refuse to convert or submit 'combatants.' Earlier this month, militants attacked the historic St. Louis de Montfort Church in Meza village, burning the church, a missionary residence, and a Catholic kindergarten. Bishop António Juliasse Ferreira Sandramo of Pemba described it as 'a scene of genuine terror.' The insurgency has killed thousands and displaced more than a million people over nine years. The world calls the victims civilians. IS-M calls them combatants. The Bible calls them conquerors.[1]

Jesus warned His disciples: "And do not fear those who kill the body but cannot kill the soul. But rather fear Him who is able to destroy both soul and body in hell" (Matthew 10:28). The IS-M militant with a machete can separate a Christian's head from their body in Cabo Delgado. He cannot separate that Christian from Christ. He can burn a church built by missionaries. He cannot burn the church Christ built. He can call a believer a combatant. He cannot make that believer take up a sword. The fear of the Lord is the antidote to the fear of men, and the Christians of Namecala village learned this at the edge of a blade.

And do not fear those who kill the body but cannot kill the soul. But rather fear Him who is able to destroy both soul and body in hell.

Matthew 10:28

Jesus spoke to the church in Smyrna: "Be faithful until death, and I will give you the crown of life" (Revelation 2:10). The crown of life is not a metaphor for survival. It is a promise to the dying. The two Christians beheaded near Namecala on May 8 did not survive. They were faithful until death. The five killed in the village on May 9 did not outlast the militants. They outlasted the fear of death. The crown of life is not given to those who avoid martyrdom. It is given to those who refuse to renounce Christ in the face of it. IS-M believes it is executing combatants. It is only crowning conquerors.

John saw the martyrs under the altar in heaven: "They cried with a loud voice, saying, 'How long, O Lord, holy and true, until You judge and avenge our blood on those who dwell on the earth?'" (Revelation 6:10). This is not a prayer for personal revenge. It is a prayer for divine justice. The blood of the Namecala martyrs cries out from Mozambican soil. The blood of the Meza church congregation cries out from the ashes of St. Louis de Montfort. The Lord who heard Abel's blood will not fail to hear theirs. The militant who swings the machete believes he is writing history. He is only writing his own indictment. The Judge of all the earth will by no means clear the guilty. He will repay.

The world has already forgotten Namecala. The UN will issue a statement. The Rwandan troops will patrol. The energy companies will calculate the risk to their natural gas projects. And the church in Cabo Delgado will still meet on Sunday — diminished, displaced, but defiant. Because the church does not fear those who kill the body. The church fears the One who can destroy both soul and body in hell — and the church belongs to Him. The Christians of northern Mozambique are not victims of a resource conflict or a failed state. They are witnesses to the sovereignty of God, the same God who numbers the hairs on the heads of the beheaded, who bottles the tears of the widowed, and who reserves a crown of life for every saint who was faithful until death. The militants believe they are ending stories. They are only writing footnotes to a triumph they cannot see.

Two Christians beheaded. Five more killed. A church and 160 homes burned. IS-M calls them combatants for refusing to renounce Christ. The Bible calls them conquerors. The crown of life is not a metaphor. It is what Jesus gives to those who are faithful until death.

Not a devotional. Not a church bulletin. Not a news aggregator. We're not pastors, theologians, or influencers. We don't chase trends or write for engagement. We write for readers who don't yet share the frame, alongside those who do.
