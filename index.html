Reformed theology for a shaken world — Scripture-grounded. Confessionally rooted.

May 18, 2026

Union with Christ is not a metaphor. It is the channel through which every blessing flows.

Ask the average Christian how they are saved, and you will hear a list. Ask what connects those blessings to their actual life, and the answer gets quieter.

All Scripture is breathed out by God and profitable for teaching, for reproof, for correction, and for training in righteousness.

2 Timothy 3:16

Ask the average Christian how they are saved, and you will hear a list: forgiven, justified, adopted, sanctified, glorified. Ask the average Christian what connects those blessings to their actual life, and the answer gets quieter. The Bible has a single answer, and it is so central that Paul returns to it roughly 160 times. He calls it being in Christ. Not a metaphor. Not a feeling. A real, spiritual, personal bond that makes every other blessing possible.

John 15 is the most direct statement of this connection in the New Testament. Jesus does not say, "Learn from me," or "Follow my example," or even "Believe my teachings." He says, "Abide in me." The Greek word menō means to remain, to dwell, to stay. It is the same word used in John 1:14 for the Word "dwelling" among us. Union with Christ is the continuation of the Incarnation — not in the flesh, but in the Spirit. The same Spirit who conceived Christ in Mary's womb now conceives Christ in the believer's heart. That is why Paul can say, Galatians 2:20. He is not speaking poetically. He is describing a real, personal indwelling.

John 15:5 makes the organic nature of this connection unmistakable. The branch does not receive sap, sunlight, and stability from three separate sources. It receives everything from the vine. You are not forgiven and then joined to Christ. You are forgiven because you are joined to Christ. You are not sanctified and then adopted. You are sanctified and adopted because you are in Him. The order is not sequential. It is organic. The branch does not negotiate with the vine. It abides.

But this connection is not only organic. It is also legal. 2 Corinthians 5:21 says God treated Jesus as a sinner so that He could treat us as if we were righteous. Just as Adam was the head of the human race whose disobedience made many sinners, so Christ is the head of His people whose obedience is credited to those who are in Him. Romans 5:19 is not abstract theology. It is the transfer of a debt. The debt was real. The payment was real. The credit is real. And it is ours because we are in Him.

And because of him you are in Christ Jesus, who became to us wisdom from God, righteousness and sanctification and redemption.

1 Corinthians 1:30

The pastoral implication is severe and comforting. Severe, because it means you cannot have some of Christ. You cannot have His forgiveness without His lordship, His peace without His Spirit, His heaven without His holiness. The branch does not negotiate with the vine. Comforting, because it means you cannot lose some of Christ either. If you are in Him, you are in Him wholly — justified, adopted, indwelt, sealed, and kept. Colossians 3:3-4 says your life is hidden with Christ in God. Hidden means secure. It means the world cannot see it, the devil cannot reach it, and death cannot end it. It is tucked inside the life of the Son, and the Son is tucked inside the Father. The believer is in Christ, Christ is in the Father, and the Spirit is in the believer — a trinitarian nesting doll of security.

Romans 8:38-39 is the guarantee. The connection is not maintained by your grip on Christ. It is maintained by His grip on you. The branch does not work at remaining in the vine. It simply remains. Abiding is not striving. It is staying. The striving belongs to the Vine-keeper, who prunes, protects, and feeds. The branch's job is to receive. That is not passivity. It is the most active dependence there is.

The application is not technique. You do not achieve this connection through a method, a prayer, or a spiritual discipline. You receive it through faith, and you experience it through the ordinary means of grace: the preached Word, the sacraments, and prayer. Ephesians 1:3 says every spiritual blessing is already yours in Christ. Not someday. Not if you perform well enough. Now. The blessing is not waiting at the end of a journey. It is the vehicle in which the journey takes place.

You are not forgiven and then joined to Christ. You are forgiven because you are joined to Christ. Every blessing flows from one source: the vine.

Four angles on the ground beneath your feet.

Covenant

The Bible is not sixty-six books that happen to share a binding.

Most readers treat the Bible like an anthology. The Bible itself has never read it that way.
Justice

Thirty-six nations signed a treaty to prosecute Putin. God signed none. He didn't need to.

Thirty-six nations created a special tribunal at The Hague to prosecute Vladimir Putin for the crime of aggression against Ukraine. Romans 13 says the magistrate bears the sword as God's minister. Amos 1 says God judges nations for war crimes without waiting for a signed treaty. Every human court is provisional. Every judge will be judged.
Family

Some parents break their children with harshness. Some break them with neglect. The Bible forbids both.

Sixty years of secular research landed on a formula: warmth plus boundaries equals flourishing kids. Proverbs wrote it down 3,000 years ago. Ephesians added what the research cannot: children are image-bearers AND sinners, and the only power that sustains loving authority is grace.
Ecclesiology

Rome threatened excommunication. The SSPX shrugged. Neither asked the right question.

The Vatican issued a final warning to SSPX: consecrate four new bishops without papal consent and incur automatic excommunication. The SSPX plans to proceed. Both sides assume someone other than Christ is Head of the church. The Reformation called this wrong in 1521. It's still wrong.
Persecution

They walked into a conflict zone to make peace. They were killed doing it.

Three Kuki Baptist church leaders were killed in Manipur while carrying a mandate for reconciliation between ethnic rivals. Jesus said peacemakers are called sons of God. The world kills them. God calls them family.
Ecclesiology

Egypt legalized 191 churches. Christ authorized one.

Egypt approved legal status for 191 more churches this week. Good news. But the church of Jesus Christ does not need Cairo's permission to exist. Christ authorized the church at Calvary, commissioned it at the Great Commission, and will present it to Himself in glory. No government participates in any of those acts.
Cultural Engagement

Silicon Valley found religion. It didn't find God.

The inaugural Faith-AI Covenant roundtable in New York brought together Anthropic, OpenAI, and representatives from Christian, Hindu, Sikh, Buddhist, and Baha'i traditions. The agenda: infuse morality into AI. Tech is turning to morality. It has not turned to God. The difference is everything.
Ecclesiology

45 denominations. One federation. 834 reasons why.

Forty-five church leaders across Catholic, Protestant, Evangelical, and Pentecostal traditions formed the National Federation of Churches in India — the first body of its kind in the nation's history. Anti-Christian attacks surged from 151 in 2014 to 834 in 2024. Persecution forced unity that theology could not.

What it isn't

Not a devotional. Not a church bulletin. Not a news aggregator. We're not pastors, theologians, or influencers. We don't chase trends or write for engagement. We write for readers who don't yet share the frame, alongside those who do.

How it's checked

Every piece is sourced against Scripture and primary texts, read as a secularist would read it, and checked against the experience of someone who left an abusive church. If it fails any gate, it goes back to draft. Read how we verify.
